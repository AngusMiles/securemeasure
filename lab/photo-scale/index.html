Photo scale lab

Checks two ways to get a door's real size from one photo, with no app and no paper: the iPhone's own focus distance (stored inside Pro iPhone photos), and the brick courses beside the door. Nothing is uploaded: everything happens on this phone.

Do this first: the 10-minute check

You need a Pro iPhone (12 Pro or later: it has a small black dot, the LiDAR sensor, next to the cameras).

  1. Open the iPhone Camera app. Photo mode, 1x, phone held upright.
  2. Stand 2 to 3 metres from a front door with the door in the middle of the screen. Do not tap the screen. Take one photo.
  3. In the Photos app, open that photo, tap Share, then Save to Files.
  4. Back here, type a door name in step 1 (for example front-door), then give the photo each way in the list, one after the other: the three Photo Library choices (they differ only in which file types the page says it accepts), Files, In-page camera, and Take Photo.
  5. After each one, read the green or red box that says whether the MakerNote and the AF depth came through. The table at the bottom fills in by itself.
  6. For the error table, check the 4 dots in step 2 on each photo: move a dot, or tick "the 4 dots are on the corners". Photos with unchecked dots are left out.
  7. At the next door, type its name before giving its first photo: the photo on screen keeps the door it was given with.

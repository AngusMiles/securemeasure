Door gauge in Apple AR

This page makes a life-size measuring gauge and opens it in the iPhone's built-in AR viewer (AR Quick Look, part of iOS). Nothing to install, no app. You hold the gauge against a real door and read it, then compare with a tape measure. Nothing leaves this phone unless you copy or share it in step 5.

2. Make the gauge and open it in AR

  1. Choose a gauge (start with A, the tape L).
  2. Leave the other choices as they are for your first run.
  3. Tap Make the AR link, then tap the blue Open in AR button.
  4. If the viewer opens on Object, tap AR at the top. Point the phone at the wall beside the door and move it slowly side to side until the gauge appears on the wall.
  5. Drag the gauge so its red zero (the outline's corner on C and D) sits on the bottom-left inside corner of the door opening.
  6. Read the gauge first (see step 3 for how) and remember or jot down the readings.
  7. Only then try a pinch, and note whether the size changes. Readings taken after a pinch resized it do not count (if you need to read again, pinch back until it shows 100 %).
  8. Close the AR view and fill in step 3.

3. After AR: write down what happened

How to read the gauge
  • Stand square-on to each edge and hold the phone level with it. For the head (top) reading, raise the phone to the head. The gauge sits on whatever surface the phone found, and the wall face can be 50-150 mm in front of the door frame, so reading at an angle shifts the number.
  • On gauges A, B and E the numbers are centimetres, one every 10 cm (label 200 = 2000 mm). On A and E each small tick is 10 mm: label 200 + 4 ticks = 2040 mm, and an edge a little past that 4th tick is about 2043. On B estimate the mm between the 100 mm bars. On C and D the numbers are sizes in mm.
  • Write every reading in millimetres.
  • Note whether it stuck to the wall or the door itself.

4. Product flow: photo, then pick the size in AR

  1. Measure the opening with a tape and enter it below (or after).
  2. Tap Photo the door, allow the camera, frame the whole door and tap Take photo.
  3. Check the four corner dots sit on the inside corners of the opening. Drag any that are off.
  4. Tap Make the outline links. Open each outline in AR. On the one that fits the door, tap This size fits at the bottom of the AR view.
  5. Back here, check the pick was recorded, then tap Save flow row.

The outlines stick to the wall standing up (gauge D's normal choice), with step 2's scale lock and file choice. Tick the box below only to test step 2's "Sticks to" and "Which way" here too.

Measure a door by sliding the phone

This test page tries to measure a door in millimetres with only the camera and the phone's motion sensors: you film the door while sliding the phone left and right, and it works out the size. You then compare its answer with a tape measure.

1 About this door (once per door)

Measure the opening (inside the frame), floor or sill to the top.

Optional: camera-height test

Heights are personal: they stay on this phone. Unless you tick this box, Download and Share leave out the heights and the camera-height sizes worked out from them, and Copy keeps only those sizes' errors against the tape.

2 Choose the test

3 Lens check (optional)

Takes one photo to read the lens's focal length from it (iPhone with iOS 18.4 or newer). Do it once per phone, before measuring.

4 Measure

  1. Tap Start. Allow motion and camera when asked.
  2. Stand 2 to 2.5 m back. Hold the phone upright with both hands and fit the whole door inside the white frame.
  3. When the green outline sits on the door opening, tap Yes. If it is wrong, tap Not right (or tap the 4 corners yourself).
  4. Hold still for 1 second.
  5. Slide the phone left and right about a hand-width (15 to 30 cm), about once a second, keeping it pointing straight at the door, until the ring is full (about 7 s).
  6. Hold still again. The result appears below.
